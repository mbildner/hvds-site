Hexchaser Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

About Hexchaser

Hexchaser is a single-player puzzle game for iOS made by Hudson Valley Data Solutions LLC. The game runs entirely on your device and does not connect to the internet.

What we collect

Nothing. Hexchaser does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your game settings and best scores are saved only on your device so the game can remember them between sessions. This information never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it entirely.

Children's privacy

Because Hexchaser collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hexchaser ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
